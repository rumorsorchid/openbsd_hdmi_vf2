Hi,

arm64 GENERIC has simplefb(4), riscv64 GENERIC does not.  On riscv64
boards whose firmware sets up a display and hands it over through the
EFI GOP, efiboot already creates the simple-framebuffer node, but
nothing attaches to it.

On a StarFive VisionFive 2 (JH7110) with U-Boot that brings up HDMI
itself (https://github.com/rumorsorchid/openbsd_hdmi_vf2), the diff
below gives a wsdisplay on HDMI, and Xorg runs with the stock wsfb
driver.  The firmware hands the framebuffer over at the SoC's uncached
view of DRAM, so the kernel needs no cache handling for it.  The
kernel console stays on the serial port.

Tested on 7.9-stable [and -current, if you tried it].

Index: sys/arch/riscv64/conf/GENERIC
--- sys/arch/riscv64/conf/GENERIC
+++ sys/arch/riscv64/conf/GENERIC
@@ -44,6 +44,10 @@
 # NS16550 compatible serial ports
 com*		at fdt?
 
+# Framebuffer set up by the firmware
+simplefb*	at fdt?
+wsdisplay*	at simplefb?
+
 # Allwinner SoCs
 sxiccmu*	at fdt? early 1
 sxidog*		at fdt?
