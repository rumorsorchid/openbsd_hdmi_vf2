Hi,

On the StarFive VisionFive 2 (JH7110), the HFI BIOS firmware
(https://gitlab.com/bios-riscv/hfi-bios, U-Boot based, GPL) brings up
HDMI and provides an EFI GOP framebuffer.  efiboot turns that into a
simple-framebuffer node, and simplefb(4) attaches fine, but riscv64
GENERIC does not include simplefb, unlike arm64.

The first diff adds it.

With simplefb enabled, the picture on the JH7110 is corrupt: writes sit in
the L2 cache and the DC8200 display controller, which is not coherent,
scans stale memory.  The U74 cores have no Svpbmt, so PMAP_NOCACHE does
not help.  The JH7110 has an uncached alias of DRAM at DRAM + 16 GiB
(0x4_4000_0000, physical address bit 34).
The second diff makes simplefb use that alias on the JH7110.  With it,
both the console and Xorg (stock wsfb, ShadowFB) work at 1920x1080.

[Describe your result honestly: console speed, X speed, anything odd.]

If a quirk in simplefb is not the right place, the firmware could instead
report the alias address in its GOP; I am happy to take it there.

Tested on a VisionFive 2 v1.3B, 8 GB, OpenBSD 7.9-stable [and -current].
dmesg below.
